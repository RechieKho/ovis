# unclear span each, and the reading a person means. Measures the ranking
# (M0.4: where the right reading lands among the candidates) and the model
# (M0.2, M0.3: how often it picks it, how sure it is, how fast). The M1.14
# programs will add to these.
#

# `expect: none` means no reading fits: the build should ask the author.
# Words used here are kept out of the library's aliases, or the line would
# need no question.
